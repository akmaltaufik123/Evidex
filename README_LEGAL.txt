2. MALAYSIA CYBER CRIME & LEGAL FRAMEWORK
   Users operating this tool within Malaysia (or subject to international 
   cyber jurisdictions) must comply with local statutory regulations:
   
   * Computer Crimes Act 1997 (Act 563): 
     - Section 3 penalizes unauthorized access to computer material. 
       Misusing credentials or harvesting private data without proper 
       authorization is a punishable offense.
     - Section 6 penalizes wrongful communication of access codes/passwords.
   * Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA): 
     - Governs the processing of commercial and personal data. Unauthorized 
     harvesting and distribution of personal identifiable information (PII) 
     can result in severe legal penalties, heavy fines, and imprisonment.
   * Penal Code & Communications and Multimedia Act 1998 (CMA): 
     - Prohibits fraudulent or improper use of network facilities and 
     unauthorized communications tracking or interception.
