# Official reporting directory

Contact details are held in `contacts` in `evidex_next_content.json`, with
bilingual interface copy in `ms` and `en`. Checked 30 October 2026. This is a
reference list, not a comprehensive bank registry or a government integration.
Users have the right to review and choose evidence, recipients and attachments.
No data is sent automatically. `mailto:` opens the user's mail application with
only the address, without a subject, body, credentials, file paths or attachments.
`tel:` opens the device's dialer where supported. External HTTPS links use
noopener/noreferrer and no-referrer. Logos are local assets.

## Sources and scope

- SKMM complaint portal, hotline and complaint email: official local-government
  notice https://mdcameron.gov.my/en/node/1882 . MCMC's own site returned 403
  during verification; this government notice dated October 2026 was used.
- PDRM main line and general email:
  https://www.rmp.gov.my/direktori/talian-bilik-gerakan and
  https://rmp2u.rmp.gov.my/content/hubungi-kami?lang=en . General email is labelled
  as enquiries, not a formal police-report channel. Criminal reports should be
  lodged at a station: https://ereporting.rmp.gov.my/index.aspx . 999 is for
  immediate danger; Bukit Aman operations-room line is separately labelled.
- Cyber999 incident email, reporting form, office hotline and emergency line:
  https://www.cybersecurity.my/portal-main/services/cyber999-overview . The
  emergency mobile number is explicitly limited to DDoS, phishing and data breach.
- NSRC 997: https://www.bnm.gov.my/faqs/scams and
  https://nfcc.jpm.gov.my/index.php/en/about-nsrc . Do not wait for acquisition
  before calling the bank or NSRC. No NSRC email is fabricated. Operating hours
  differ across official source pages, so hours are not asserted in this directory.
- Commercial bank contact list (13 entries including two OCBC departments):
  https://www.abm.org.my/safe-online-banking/directory/ . Citibank is omitted
  rather than inferring whether the listed legacy retail contact remains suitable.
- Bank Islam: https://www.bankislam.com/wp-content/uploads/FAQ-New-Safety-Measures-1.pdf
- BSN: https://www.bsn.com.my/page/sms-phone-scam?lang=en
- Bank Rakyat: https://www.irakyat.com.my/portal-main/faq
- Agrobank: https://www.agrobank.com.my/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/BM-RIB-FAQ-03122025.pdf

The four agency cards and 17 bank/department entries include source links.
Re-check contacts against these sources before future releases. Users are also
shown the verification date and directed to their bank card or official bank
website if a contact is missing or changes. No guarantees of agency acceptance,
response time, court admissibility or endorsement are made.
